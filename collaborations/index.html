How collaborations are selected

The board contains concrete data-focused work, not a directory of interesting organizations. Evidence and attribution must match Conjecture 56's actual role.

  • Current: recent evidence supports the stated status, available work, and delivered outcomes.
  • Clear: ownership, attribution, participation conditions, and Conjecture 56's role are explicit.
  • Bounded: a useful contribution that can be completed in approximately two to four weeks.
  • Verifiable: a visible outcome with licensing, assignment, domain, and data constraints understood.

Competitions, formal jobs, inactive repositories, generic communities, and speculative unsolicited projects are excluded.
